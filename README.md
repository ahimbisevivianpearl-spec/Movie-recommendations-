# Movie-recommendations-
Thrillers
The story — full spoilers
1. CW has survived!

Remember how Madison escaped and CW was left behind at the end of the first movie? CW somehow survives and starts a new life in southern France. She has a girlfriend named Diane, and they seem happy together.

2. Another influencer becomes her target.

They meet Charlotte, a demanding influencer who disrupts their anniversary getaway. CW becomes irritated with her, takes her to a secluded location and pushes her to her death. Then she steals Charlotte's online identity and pretends she's still alive.

3. Diane discovers the truth.

Diane finds fake passports and evidence connecting CW to previous murders. She realises her girlfriend is a killer and confronts her.

CW panics and kills Diane, too. But instead of letting go, she uses AI to recreate Diane's voice, creating a digital version of the woman she murdered. Yes, girl, it's that disturbing. 
IMDb+1


4. Madison comes back to find CW.

Madison has been struggling because people still associate her with the murders CW committed. When she learns about Charlotte's death, she investigates and follows the trail to Bali, determined to expose CW.

There, they both become involved with Jacob, a toxic online personality, and his girlfriend Ariana. CW hacks Jacob's computer and leaks a compromising video involving him and Ariana.

The fallout is devastating: Ariana dies by suicide, and Jacob blames Madison.

5. The final confrontation is INSANE.

Madison and CW finally face each other. Their confrontation turns violent, and Jacob gets caught up in the chaos.

CW eventually stabs Jacob to death while his livestream is running. Because Madison has arranged for the footage to be broadcast, CW's crimes are exposed to a massive online audience.

But CW doesn't calmly surrender. She launches into another violent rampage, attacking Jacob's friend and the people accompanying him as the film ends. 
Plot Explained+1

My verdict
The first movie is about stealing someone's life. The sequel takes that idea further with murder, revenge, deepfakes and AI.

The biggest difference: Madison is no longer just trying to survive CW. She's actively trying to bring her down.

And honestly? If you like beautiful holiday destinations hiding ugly secrets, this sequel is right up your alley. Just be prepared for much more graphic violence.
